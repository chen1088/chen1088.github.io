About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University at Buffalo, advised by Kenneth Regan.

My research interests include algorithms, computational complexity, automata theory, and combinatorics. My current work has a particular focus on the Separating Words Problem: how many states does a finite automaton need to accept one word and reject another?

Research interests

I work on algorithm design and analysis, computational complexity, and parameterized complexity. My projects include pattern problems in matrices and the state complexity of separating words. In automata theory, I am interested in new constructions, elementary proofs of separation bounds, and algebraic explanations of the limitations of restricted automata.
