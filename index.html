About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University at Buffalo, advised by Kenneth Regan.

My research interests include algorithms, complexity, automata theory, and combinatorics. My long term research work is analytic framework of syntactic monoids and their products. My current PhD thesis has a particular focus on the Separating Words Problem: how many states does a DFA need to accept one word and reject another?

Research taste

I developed a taste alongside of my research journey, favoring approaches that are elementary, minimalist, and irregular.

Elementary
I ensure every patient high-school student can understand my proofs.
Minimalist
I try my best to simplify my proofs without compressing them.
Irregular
Favoring irregularity was intensified as I wrote a lot of code to find counterexamples and patterns.
